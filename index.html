WHO WE ARE

Engineered with precision.

Duas Unique Services Limited is a premier Nigerian multidisciplinary engineering and technical contracting company delivering integrated civil, energy, telecom, and procurement solutions.

Headquartered in the Federal Capital Territory, Abuja, we blend technical rigor, heavy plant capacity, and disciplined project governance. Our cross-functional teams handle everything from complex highway rehabilitations and commercial structural frameworks to community solar mini-grids and federal procurement contracts.
